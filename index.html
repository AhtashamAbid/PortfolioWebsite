Building immersive Augmented Reality experiences that blend the digital and physical worlds seamlessly. Working with Unity AR Foundation to create interactive AR applications featuring 3D object placement, plane detection, image tracking, and spatial mapping. Developing AR solutions for mobile platforms (iOS & Android) with optimized performance, realistic lighting, and intuitive gesture controls. Exploring the future of mixed reality where virtual objects interact naturally with real-world environments, creating engaging experiences that push the boundaries of what's possible in AR development.

Unity AR AR Foundation Mobile Mixed Reality

🎮 Unity 2D & 3D Games

I build and prototype 2D and 3D games in Unity with a strong focus on feel — responsive input, tight movement, satisfying combat, and clear feedback. From core gameplay systems (state machines, abilities, AI, UI) to polish (animation, VFX, camera, audio hooks) and performance (profiling, pooling, mobile-friendly optimizations), I enjoy taking ideas from a playable prototype to a polished experience.

Unity 2D 3D Gameplay Optimization

🥽 Unity VR Development

I develop VR experiences in Unity focused on comfort and immersion — smooth locomotion options, interaction systems (grab, UI, tools), and reliable performance. I work with XR rigs, tracked controllers, physics-based interactions, and scene optimization (LOD, baked lighting, draw call reduction) to keep frame rate stable and the experience comfortable across headsets.

Unity VR XR Interaction Performance

☕ Cafe Craft

A 2D Android game built entirely from scratch! Players manage a cozy shop where customers place orders for items you craft by merging similar objects to create higher-tier dishes. Each unlock dynamically changes what customers order! Designed & developed 100% by me — from concept art to code. Built in Unity (C#) with all characters fully rigged and animated in 2D using bones & splines. Features dynamic order generation based on unlocked counters and optimized for Android/iOS with smooth gameplay and responsive UI. The most fun part was creating a system where every shop expansion (like unlocking the bakery) automatically adds new recipe chains and new customer order logic.

Unity C# 2D Android iOS
